01 · Overview & Data Privacy

1. What does this app do?

  • Calculates scheme-accurate 1RMs & working weights: Prescribes your starting working weights and reps for each set from your rolling 7-day or 28-day 1RM across single-stage schemes (3×5, 3×8, 3×10, 4×10, Drop Sets), multi-day/week periodized schemes (Prilepin's Table, 5/3/1 BBB, Texas Method, DUP, RPT, GVT, GZCLP), and custom multi-stage schemes.
  • Tracks bodyweight, free-weight, and multi-gym machine strength: Logs bodyweight sessions with added or assisted load, deduces provisional starting loads for new movements from your muscle-group reference lift, and auto-recalibrates machine/cable pulley ratios across different gyms.
  • Syncs with your own Google Sheet: Keeps a complete, editable calendar history of your sessions, notes, and 1RM trends on your device and syncs them to a dedicated tab in your personal Google Sheet.

2. What doesn’t this app do?

  • It can’t predict your future gains or spot an off day: The prescribed weights are calculated strictly from your recent logged history—not a guarantee of how strong you'll feel today. You still need to listen to your body, figure out the right working weight for that day using the − / + buttons in 02 Log, or toggle 🌙 Off-day: Log only (Skip 1RM) when you are fatigued.
  • No external servers or third-party accounts: It does not upload your workouts, profile, or Google Sheet URL to any external company database or proprietary cloud.
  • No silent overwrites of your routines, completed sets, or spreadsheet tabs: Swapping a lift in 02 Log only affects today's workout (your saved routines in 04 Setup stay locked until you tap ✎ Edit Setup and ✓ Confirm), changing settings mid-workout never alters sets you've already checked off, and syncing never clears your other Google Sheet tabs.
  • No cardio, nutrition, or social clutter: It does not track calories, macros, GPS runs, or social feeds—it focuses strictly on strength programming, set logging, and 1RM progression.

3. How is my data stored?

  • Local-first in your browser (localStorage): Every set, note, routine, custom scheme, and 1RM update is saved immediately to your browser's local storage on your device, so the app works offline and never loses progress if a connection drops.
  • Synced to a dedicated form_app_data tab in your Google Sheet: When connected via your Google Apps Script Web App (/exec) URL, the app reads and writes structured rows (SETTING, GYM, MOVEMENT, ROUTINE, SCHEME, and LOG) exclusively inside a worksheet tab named form_app_data in your own Google Drive spreadsheet—giving you full ownership, backup, and spreadsheet access to your training history without touching any other tabs in your file.
02 · Getting Started & Google Sheet Sync

4. How do I connect my Google Sheet?

In Step 1 of the Setup Wizard (accessible anytime via ⏻ Log Out / Setup Wizard in 06 Settings), paste your Google Sheet URL, copy the 30-second Apps Script into Extensions → Apps Script in your sheet, deploy it as a Web App (Who has access: Anyone), and paste the /exec URL. Tap 🔍 Verify Read/Write & Check Sheet to confirm access and either load your existing records or calibrate starting 1RMs.

5. What do the sync status badges mean?

  • ⏳ Pending: Saved locally on your device; waiting for your Google Sheet to confirm the write.
  • ✓ Confirmed: Your Google Sheet verified that all rows were written to form_app_data.
  • ⚠️ Sync failed: Saved locally on your device, but the Google Sheet couldn't be reached; tap ↑ Sync to Sheet Now in 06 Settings to retry.
  • ○ Disconnected: Local-only mode; no network requests are sent until you reconnect. When connected, the app automatically pulls the latest data and active routine from your Sheet whenever you open or refocus the app.

6. How do I estimate my starting 1RMs during setup if I haven't tested a 1-rep max?

In Step 2 of the Setup Wizard (or in 02 Log → ⚡ 1RM Formula Test), you can either choose ⚡ Direct Formula Test and enter any weight and rep count you can lift (Weight × (1 + Reps / 30)), or pick any training Scheme, tap the Week / Day / Stage pill you use, and enter your working weight for that stage's fixed target reps.

03 · Logging Workouts & 1RM Calculation

7. How does the app calculate my 1RM from different workout schemes?

Every set in a scheme is evaluated against its own prescribed %1RM and target reps:

Set 1RM = (Total Load ÷ Set %1RM) × ln(1 + Actual Reps) ÷ ln(1 + Target Reps)

Your session 1RM for that movement is the average across all prescribed sets in that scheme.

8. What happens if I skip a set or don't finish all prescribed sets in a scheme?

Once you check (✓) at least one set on an exercise, any unchecked set is treated as 0 total load (Set 1RM = 0)—equivalent to 0 kg/lbs on free weights or −Bodyweight on bodyweight lifts—and averaged across all prescribed sets. Dropping the weight on a fatigued set and completing your reps is always rewarded over skipping the set. If you cut a workout short for non-strength reasons, switch the movement to 🌙 Off-day: Log only (Skip 1RM).

9. How are bodyweight exercises (like Pull-Ups) calculated and stored?

Total system load is Session Bodyweight + Added Weight − Assistance Weight. Enter positive numbers (+10) for added weight, 0 for unweighted bodyweight, and negative numbers (-15) for assisted sets. Each logged set stores your Session Bodyweight and ± External Weight (Added or Assistance Weight) in 03 Records.

04 · Progression, Untested Lifts & Multi-Gym Calibration

10. When do multi-day or multi-week schemes automatically advance to the next step?

  • Every Session (Prilepin's Table, Texas Method 5×5, DUP 3-Day): Advances to the next Day (D1 → D2 → D3) immediately after you complete all prescribed sets.
  • Weekly Wave (5/3/1 BBB, RPT Pyramid, German Volume GVT): Advances once per calendar week (holding steady on the current week if you train that lift multiple times in the same week, and pausing without skipping weeks if you take a week off).
  • Performance Stage (GZCLP Tier 1 & Tier 2): Stays on the current stage when you hit all target reps as your 1RM increases, and advances to the next stage (5×3+ → 6×2+ or 3×10 → 3×8) only when you miss a target rep or skip a set.

11. Why do some movements show a Provisional load badge?

Each muscle group in 06 Settings → Movement Estimator has 1 Reference Lift. Any untested movement in that muscle group whose 1RM is auto-deduced from the reference lift (with a -15% conservative buffer) displays Provisional load until you log a workout for it or enter a direct 1RM override.

12. How does the app calibrate machines and cables across different gyms?

Barbells, dumbbells, and bodyweight lifts share the same 1RM across all gyms. Machines and cables auto-recalibrate via 3 rules as you log in 02 Log:

  1. Same gym → Same gym: Consecutive sessions on a machine at the same gym transfer your % strength gain to all other saved gyms so inactive gyms keep pace with your progress.
  2. Switching to a new gym: Your first session on a machine at a new gym sets that gym's 1RM and calibrates its pulley ratio without changing your other gyms.
  3. Switching back to a previous gym: Logging after switching back updates that gym's 1RM directly and auto-corrects the pulley ratio between the stacks.

13. How do I edit a past workout or fix a mistaken 1RM entry?

In 03 Records, select the date on the calendar and tap ✎ Edit on the movement card to change set weights, reps, or notes (or tap 🗑️ Remove Movement), then tap ✓ Save & Update 1RM to recalculate your 1RM and trend chart—or tap ↩ Undo to reverse your last action.
